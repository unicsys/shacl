Why This Canvas Matters:
The central column (Information Things) is where all your data lives - measurements, reports, identifiers, descriptions. But data without context is meaningless.
The surrounding columns transform data into knowledge:
•	Left columns (Material Things, Qualities/Functions/Roles): Define WHAT exists in your domain - the real-world entities, their properties, and the roles they play
•	Right columns (Processes, Time Periods): Capture HOW and WHEN things happen - the dynamics and temporal aspects of your domain
This structure creates a shared semantic framework - a common language that bridges three critical gaps:
1.	Human-to-Human: Different teams (engineering, operations, maintenance) share the same mental model
2.	Human-to-Machine: Domain experts' knowledge becomes computationally accessible
3.	Machine-to-Machine: Different systems and AI algorithms interpret data consistently
Without this semantic context, your data is just numbers and text. With this ontology structure, every piece of data carries its full business meaning - what it describes, how it relates to other entities, and why it matters for decisions.
The result: When a domain expert asks a question, when an algorithm processes data, and when AI generates insights, they all operate with the exact same understanding. This isn't just about organizing data - it's about encoding institutional knowledge in a form that scales across people, systems, and time.
The canvas is the blueprint for turning isolated data points into connected, contextual, actionable intelligence.

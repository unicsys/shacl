🛰️ Title / Introduction (Top Quadrant)

Ontology-Driven Data Integration & Contract-Based Framework for Autonomous Decision Support in Space Systems

Selling Point:
Explainable, data-integrated framework for Battle Management Command, Control & Communication (BMC3) — enabling runtime monitoring, fault detection, and autonomous decision support across LEO satellites and ground assets.

🔹 Problem Definition & Impact (Left Quadrant)

Problem Definition

Fragmented LEO data: thermal, comms, ops, environment — siloed.

Manual analysis, delayed decisions, no traceability.

No unified reasoning layer for autonomy or mission assurance.

Current NSDC / BMC3 / JADC2 processes — slow testing, limited visibility, huge SATCOM data pipes, lack of validation.

Slowness in V&V and deployment; difficult to integrate multi-vendor solutions.

Relation to Seedling Solicitation

Supports disruptive digital engineering, trusted AI, and autonomous ops.

Impact

CRAD Targets: NASA (DT & MBSE), AFRL (resilient architectures), DARPA (BMC3 + autonomy), USSF NSDC (real-time decision support).

Qualitative: Converts data to actionable, explainable knowledge. Faster decisions, verifiable autonomy.

Quantitative: 30% faster data-to-decision; improved fault detection and V&V throughput.

Transition Path: Raytheon Space (BMC3 autonomy), Collins (LEO comms integration), PW (DT verification).

BU SMEs: Raytheon autonomy teams, Collins OCKMS, PW Digital Twin.

🔹 New Idea (Right Quadrant)

Idea:

Ontology + Knowledge Graph unify data across domains.

Contract-based layer encodes design & runtime constraints.

LLM + AI-assist for document parsing, fault reasoning, and decision traceability.

Novelty:

First to link data fusion + contract models + LLM reasoning.

Supports autonomous decision support and V&V at scale.

Enables rapid test/certification of vendor solutions (aligned with Space Force BMC3 goals).

Value Beyond Current Practice:

Explainable autonomy; composable, scalable, vendor-neutral.

Accelerates testing and certification within NSDC / JADC2 ecosystems.

Risks / Mitigation:

Data integration complexity → start small (thermal + comms) and scale.

AI reliability → contracts provide validation layer.

Why it Succeeds:

Builds on RTX ontology & digital thread infra.

Direct alignment with Space Force V&V modernization.

Supports government efforts (Darren’s NSDC initiative, Ben Hawking’s testing infra) to create rapid-test flywheel.

Data Governance & Semantic Integration Initiative
Partnered with Data Governance to transform our data catalog into an intelligent, semantically-aware system through formal ontology integration. Using GTF Aftermarket as our pilot, we engaged stakeholders across existing catalog tools (Informatica, Axon) and assessed current enterprise data coverage. Leveraging RTX's Ontology-hub and Enterprise Ontology Suite, we're addressing semantic ambiguity, limited relationship mapping, and inconsistent classification that creates "metadata debt." The solution establishes machine-readable relationships and automated entity resolution, eliminating tribal knowledge dependencies while creating a scalable semantic governance framework that reduces time-to-insight and improves enterprise-wide data utilization.

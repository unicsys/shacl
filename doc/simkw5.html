Ontology with SPARQL Query Demonstration

Sample SPARQL Query: Find aircraft repaired by 'John Doe'

PREFIX ex: 
PREFIX rdf: 

SELECT ?aircraftLabel
WHERE {
  ?person ex:label "John Doe" .
  ?repair rdf:type ex:RepairProcess .
  ?repair ex:has_participant ?person .
  ?repair ex:has_participant ?aircraft .
  ?aircraft rdf:type ex:Aircraft .
  ?aircraft ex:label ?aircraftLabel .
}
